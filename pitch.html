Intelligence

Capture, explain, and prove every decision your AI makes — built for the EU AI Act.

The Problem

AI now decides who gets a loan, a job interview, or an insurance payout. When someone asks "why was I rejected?", most companies cannot answer — the model is a black box and nothing was logged.

From August 2026 the EU AI Act makes that illegal. High-risk AI systems must keep immutable logs, provide human-readable explanations, and prove human oversight.

Non-compliance costs up to €35M or 7% of global turnover. Building this in-house takes a team most mid-sized companies don't have.

The Solution

Orcanaut captures, explains, and proves every decision your AI makes.

One line of code in your ML pipeline streams each decision into an immutable ledger. SHAP plus an LLM turn it into a plain-language explanation. A dashboard gives risk officers bias monitoring, drift alerts, and regulator-ready exports.

Three layers, one integration:

➔

SDK / Audit API — Python, .NET, REST. PII anonymized locally before anything leaves your network

➔

Decision Ledger — append-only event store, separate schema per tenant, no UPDATE or DELETE

➔

Explainability Engine + Dashboard — SHAP turned into human-readable text, plus bias & drift monitoring

How It Works

01

Add one line to your pipeline

Install the SDK, expose a predict-only endpoint for explanations. PII is anonymized locally.

02

Decisions are logged immutably

Model version, hashed inputs, confidence, threshold, outcome — append-only, tamper-evident.

03

SHAP explains each decision

Async analysis after every record; an LLM turns the numbers into a sentence anyone can read.

04

Risk officers monitor & export

Track bias and drift, review individual cases, export regulator-ready audit files on demand.

Example

"Why was application #8F2A1C rejected?" → Orcanaut returns: declined due to a high debt-to-income ratio (42%) and short credit history — with the immutable log, model version, and SHAP factors behind it.
